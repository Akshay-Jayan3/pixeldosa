---
title: Conversion CTA
description: A closing call-to-action section with composable actions and optional reassurance.
---

## Usage

```tsx
<ConversionCTA
  alignment="center"
  title="Ready to get started?"
  description="Bring your team together and move the next idea forward."
  actions={<Button>Get started</Button>}
  note="No credit card required."
/>
```

## Props

| Prop | Type | Default | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `title` | `string` | — | CTA heading. |
| `description` | `string` | — | Optional supporting copy. |
| `actions` | `React.ReactNode` | — | Caller-supplied buttons or links. |
| `note` | `string` | — | Optional reassurance below the actions. |
| `alignment` | `"left" \| "center"` | `"left"` | Content alignment. |
| `className` | `string` | — | Extra section classes. |
