## Usage

```tsx
"use client";

import { GenerationJob } from "@/components/ui/generation-job";

<GenerationJob
  status={job.status}
  title="4 product images · 1024 × 1024"
  queue={{ position: job.queuePosition, typicalWait: "2 to 4 minutes" }}
  stage={job.stage}
  progress={job.measuredProgress}
  startedAt={job.startedAt}
  cost="40 credits"
  refundNote="Anything that fails is refunded"
  onCancel={job.cancel}
>
  {job.outputs.map((output) => <img key={output.id} src={output.url} alt="" />)}
</GenerationJob>
```

Pass `progress` only when you measure it. Without it, no bar is drawn.

## Why it exists

Reviews of image and video tools rank credits and unexplained waiting above quality.
Queues of ten to thirty minutes are normal at peak, and people are given a spinner, no
position, no estimate, and no idea what happens to their credits when a job fails.

## Features

- **The queue is a real state:** position, plus what the wait usually is at this moment.
- **No invented progress.** A bar appears only when you pass a measured `progress`.
- **Partial results show immediately.** With `status="partial"`, two finished images of
  four are two finished images, not a spinner.
- **The refund rule is stated before a failure**, and what happened to the money after
  one.
- **Nobody has to watch.** A "Tell me when it's done" checkbox, so long jobs don't hold
  someone at the screen.
- **Stop stays visible** for the whole run, and the elapsed counter ticks itself.

## Props

| Prop | Type | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `status` | `"queued" \| "running" \| "partial" \| "done" \| "failed" \| "cancelled"` | Required. |
| `title` | `string` | Required. What's being made. |
| `queue` | `{ position?, typicalWait? }` | Say what you know. |
| `stage` | `string` | "Rendering 3 of 4". Shimmers while running. |
| `progress` | `number` | Measured, 0–1. Omit and no bar is drawn. |
| `startedAt` | `number \| Date` | Drives the elapsed counter. |
| `cost` / `refundNote` | `string` | Formatted by you. |
| `error` | `{ message, refunded? }` | The reason, and what happened to the money. |
| `notify` / `onNotifyChange` | `boolean` / `(v) => void` | "Tell me when it's done". |
| `onCancel` / `onRetry` | `() => void` | Stop while active; Try again after a failure. |
| `children` | `ReactNode` | Finished outputs, shown as they land. |

## Accessibility

- A labelled `<section>` with a real heading.
- Status, queue position and failures are announced once through a polite live region —
  the ticking counter is not announced.
- The progress bar is decorative; the words carry the same information.
- The status dot's pulse stops under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## When not to use

- **Work that takes a second or two:** use `Generation Placeholder`.
- **An agent run with steps:** use `Agent Plan` with statuses.
- **A list of past jobs:** this is one job. A queue of many is a different surface.
